     - `CurrentGoalScreen.tsx` tracks `goalModalSessionSeqRef`, `goalMutationSeqRef`, `goalModalSessionId`, blurs/route resets, and `runGoalFormMutation`: stale parent requests cannot close a newly opened modal, overwrite `conflictBanner`/`Alert.alert`, or reset `isCreateGoalSubmitting` while a newer request is pending.

     - Transient reconciliation failures preserve valid `goal`/`loadedIdentity` and keep open `CreateGoalVersionModal`/`CreateGoalTransitionModal` drafts mounted, surfacing `reconciliation-retry-banner` with GET-only retry (`handleRetryReconciliation`) without resetting the open modal draft or re-sending the succeeded mutation.
     - Confirmed `401`/`403`/`404` responses are never swallowed: they clear `reconciliationState`/`goal`, close open modals, and apply the existing access/404 policy. Stale route/session GETs continue to be ignored by sequence and identity guards.
